Created KPI cards to track Total Sales, Average Sales, Total Transactions, and Quantity Ordered
Used Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Conditional Formatting for dynamic reporting and filtering
Analyzed sales performance by region, city, customer segment, shipping mode, and product category
Designed monthly trend analysis dashboards to identify order quantity patterns and business insights
Implemented interactive filters for region and year-wise data analysis (2014–2017)
Improved data interpretation and decision-making through clear visualizations and automated reporting

Project Outcome

The dashboard provides a centralized view of business performance, helping users quickly identify sales trends, top-performing regions/cities, customer behavior, and operational insights through interactive visual analytics.
